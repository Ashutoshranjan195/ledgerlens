# Plan to Land User #1 This Week

**Target Persona:** A freelance wedding or commercial photographer with a shoebox/desktop folder of unfiled receipts from Q1/Q2.

---

## 7-Day Action Plan

### Day 1: Outreach & Target Selection
- Direct message 3 local wedding/commercial photographers:
  > *"Hey [Name], quick question: how many unfiled receipts and equipment invoices are currently sitting in your desk drawer or downloads folder? I built a small offline tool that turns them into a categorized tax CSV in 60 seconds on your laptop with zero cloud uploads. Can I run 20 of your messy receipts through it while you watch?"*

### Day 2: The In-Person / Screen-Share Session
1. Bring the USB drive / clone repo on their laptop.
2. Run `make setup` and `make run`.
3. Open `60s Onboarding` page to give them an instant win.
4. Drag in 20 of their actual receipts and watch silently.

---

## Exact Questions to Ask

1. **"What did it get wrong?"**
   - Look at the review queue items together.
   - Note which vendors or line items required corrections.
2. **"Would you pay $49 one-time for this?"**
   - If yes: Hand them a Stripe link or cash receipt for the offline key right there.
   - If no: *"Why? Is it price, missing accounting software export (e.g. Quickbooks), or lack of mobile capture?"*
3. **"What would you need before you'd switch from your current method?"**
   - Identify whether CSV is enough or if they require custom CPA tax report formats.

---

## What to Observe & Log

- **Time to first value:** Did they see their first categorized expense in under 60 seconds?
- **Hesitation points:** Where did they hesitate in the UI?
- **Correction UX:** Was editing a low-confidence item in the Review Queue intuitive?
- **Offline reassurance:** Did seeing the Wi-Fi icon turned off create genuine relief around client data privacy?
